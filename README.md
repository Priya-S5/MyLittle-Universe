## 🌙 What is My Little Universe?

We save hundreds of photos...

but sometimes, the feeling behind those moments gets lost.

**My Little Universe** was created with a simple idea:

> *What if memories could be more than just files in a gallery?*

It is a personal digital image diary where you can create,
organize, personalize, and relive your memories in your own little universe.

---

---

## 🔐 Privacy & Security

Personal memories can be sensitive.

That's why privacy is an important part of the project's design philosophy.

The goal of My Little Universe is to create a personal space rather than
turning someone's memories into public content.

> **Some memories are not meant for everyone.  
> They belong to you.**
